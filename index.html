Unit Conversion

Examples of Unit Conversion

  • Length: 1 meter = 100 centimeters
  • Mass: 1 kilogram = 2.20462 pounds
  • Temperature: 32°F = 0°C
  • Velocity: 1 meter/second = 3.6 kilometers/hour
  • Energy: 1 Joule = 0.000947817 British thermal unit (BTU)
  • Electric Current: 1 Ampere = 1000 milliamperes
  • Voltage: 1 Volt = 1000 millivolts
  • Viscosity: 1 poise = 0.1 Pa·s
